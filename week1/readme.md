## [GreenThumb Garden Info](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/dataset/GreenThumb-Garden-Info/p78i-pat6/about_data)
**Summary:** This is a dataset of information regarding all GreenThumb-supported community gardens in New York City. 
**Interest + potential use:** I have a general interest in the presence and use of community gardens, and the general impact that urban agricultural initiatives have on the communities they serve. A lot of the information focuses on location and neighborhoods served, but another angle could focus on the hours of availablility. The dataset is filtered by hours of availablility on each day of the week, one option could be to pull information about which gardens are open during certain times (for example, looking at gardens that are open after standard 9-5 working hours).
